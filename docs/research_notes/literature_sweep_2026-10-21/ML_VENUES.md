# Literature sweep: top ML venues, for "PRM boosting" (frozen PRM + label-free spectral fusion with gray-box telemetry)

Date: 2026-10-21. Scope: ICLR 2025, ICML 2025, NeurIPS 2025, ICLR 2026, ICML 2026, NeurIPS 2026 (accepted lists), main track first.

## How the search was done

- I pulled the complete official accepted-paper lists (`<venue>/static/virtual/data/<venue>-orals-posters.json` on icml.cc, iclr.cc and neurips.cc) and searched their titles. Unique titles per list: ICLR 2025 3,830; ICML 2025 3,339; NeurIPS 2025 5,858; ICLR 2026 5,468; ICML 2026 6,646; NeurIPS 2026 6,223 accepted.
- I also ran OpenReview full-text search (api2 `notes/search`, filtered by venueid) for about 30 topic queries, and title-matched every paper on the "already known" list.
- Abstracts came from OpenReview, the neurips.cc poster pages and arXiv. Full text came from arXiv HTML or PMLR PDFs.
- Limitations:
  - The NeurIPS 2026 list looks incomplete. A third-party page (RIKEN AIP) reports about 7,900 acceptances, against 6,223 unique accepted titles in the official JSON.
  - OpenReview's note and PDF endpoints started returning a bot-verification challenge. I did not try to get around it, so papers whose only copy is on OpenReview (SPRM, StepBack, GeoR) are abstract-only.
- Read level: FULL means I read the method plus the relevant result tables in the paper body. ABS means abstract only.
- Every number is from the paper's own text or tables, and the source table is named.
- Already-known papers are not repeated in the main table. They appear only in the venue-update section when I found a venue you lack.

## Five-line summary

1. Closest new competitor: Mind the Gap (ICML 2026). It is a training-free localizer that tracks drops in top-K entropy over one teacher-forced pass, evaluated on ProcessBench (SLA 46.11 on GSM8K for Qwen3-8B, Table 3). It has the same access as our telemetry arm but uses no PRM and no fusion, and it reports neither ProcessBench F1 nor PRMScore.
2. Fusion-step baselines now at ICML 2026: CARE (label-free dependency graph plus 3-view tensor decomposition, very close to L-SML with tensor method-of-moments), Ising dependence-aware aggregation (EM), and OW/ISP. FUSE is also there (known paper; ICML 2026 venue now verified). None is tested on step-level reasoning data.
3. No paper at these venues fuses a frozen PRM with its backbone's telemetry without labels. PRMBench appears only in SPRM (NeurIPS 2026, a trained outcome-only PRM) and PURE (PURE-PRM-7B, PRMBench 65.3 / ProcessBench F1 57.5). Socratic-PRMBench and Hard2Verify appear nowhere.
4. Most gray-box signals in this window (CoE, DeepConf, GeoR, TokUR, Tracing the Traces, Self-Certainty, CUSUM entropy dynamics, logit-lens RHD) work at response or trace level, mostly at generation time. The step-level ones either use one pass (Mind the Gap), need many samples plus answer labels (SCA), or need the gold answer (Latent Veracity).
5. Venue updates for known papers:
   - Moved to ICML 2026: FUSE, Reward Under Attack, Pessimistic Verification.
   - Confirmed NeurIPS 2025: Know What You Don't Know.
   - ThinkPRM: TMLR, with an ICML 2026 poster slot.
   - Still only ICLR 2027 submissions, not accepted: PRISM, uPRM, VerifySteer, Cliff, MedPRMBench, LSR-Ben and others.
