# Step-labelled reasoning benchmarks: competitor overlap and contamination map

Compiled 2026-10-21 from official pages (ACL Anthology, proceedings.neurips.cc, iclr.cc), arXiv full text, Hugging Face (HF) cards and API, GitHub READMEs, and the Semantic Scholar (S2) Graph API. Nothing in the repository was modified. OpenReview pages could not be opened because they sit behind a browser challenge, which was not bypassed. Where an ICLR venue matters, the iclr.cc listing was used instead, and this is noted.

**How to read the tables**
- "Our 5 PRMs" means: Qwen2.5-Math-PRM-7B and Qwen2.5-Math-PRM-72B ("Qwen PRMs"), PURE-PRM-7B ("PURE"), RLHFlow Llama3.1-8B-PRM-Mistral-Data ("RLHFlow-Mistral"), and Math-Shepherd-Mistral-7B-PRM ("Math-Shepherd PRM").
- "Inferred" means the conclusion combines two cited facts and was not checked by matching problem IDs.
- "UNVERIFIED" means no source confirming it was found.
- Popularity has three numbers, written cites / top / use. **cites** is the S2 total citation count of the benchmark paper. **top** is the number of citing papers from 2025-2026 whose S2 venue is ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, ACL or EMNLP main (Findings and workshops excluded). **use** is the number of citing papers whose S2 citation snippet contains evaluation words ("evaluat", "benchmark", "results on", ...).
  - **top** is a lower bound. S2 has no venue for 20-30% of citing papers, and it tags most ICLR papers as arXiv; ICLR counts are near zero for that reason. **use** is a rough proxy for "reports results on" and also catches related-work mentions.
  - For PRM800K and Math-Shepherd, citations mostly cite the method, not use of the dataset as a benchmark.

## CRV: what the dataset is

- **Paper.** "Verifying Chain-of-Thought Reasoning via Its Computational Graph", Zhao, Koishekenov, Yang, Murray and Cancedda (FAIR at Meta, University of Edinburgh), arXiv 2510.09312. ICLR 2026 **Oral**, verified on iclr.cc.
- **CRV is a white-box method**, not primarily a benchmark. It trains a classifier on attribution-graph features computed with per-layer transcoders. To get (text, label, computational trace) tuples, the authors generated and labelled their own data.
- **Generation.** Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct writes the CoTs for three domains:
  - synthetic Boolean expressions;
  - synthetic Arithmetic (nested integer expressions, files per operator count 3/5/7/10, 10,000 expressions each);
  - the GSM8K **test** split.
- **Labels.**
  - Synthetic: LLM judge plus programmatic verifier, keeping only steps where the two agree.
  - GSM8K: Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct as judge, with manual review of a subset.
  - Only steps up to and including the first incorrect step are kept.
  - Error rates are very low: 0.2% of Boolean steps, 1.2% of Arithmetic steps and 6.6% of GSM8K steps are incorrect.
- **Public?** **Yes.** The traces, segmented steps and per-step labels are on HF `facebook/crv` (for example `gsm8k_expressions/gsm8k.annotated.json`, `arithmetic_expressions/arith.nt{3,5,7,10}.annotated.json`). The annotated files include the exact Llama-3 chat-formatted prefix before and after each step, which makes teacher-forced re-scoring straightforward. The license is **CC-BY-NC-4.0**. Transcoders are released at `facebook/crv-8b-instruct-transcoders`.
- **CROP's use.** CROP uses "the released annotated traces rather than regenerating": 1,500 Arithmetic traces and 1,319 GSM8K traces. Its 8,995 GSM8K steps differ from CRV's own reported 8,737 (counting rule not checked).

## Summary

1. The most matched comparisons come from **PRMBench** (4 of 6 competitors: PRISM, CROP, ARES, SLIDER) and **ProcessBench** (3 of 6: PRISM, CROP, VerifySteer), both ACL 2025 and the most cited of the step benchmarks: 136 and 352 S2 cites, 36 and 72 in 2025-26 main top-venue papers.
2. Next come **Hard2Verify** (ACL 2026; VerifySteer; it evaluates the Qwen PRMs) and **CRV GSM8K/Arithmetic** (CROP only, but public, natural Llama-3.1-8B errors, and ready for teacher-forced telemetry). Qwen2.5-Math-PRM-7B is the PRM most competitors share with us.
3. Main contamination risk: **PRMBench is built from PRM800K solutions, so PURE-PRM-7B (trained on PRM800K) is in-sample there**, as are CROP's PRM800K split and PRISM's own model. Math-Shepherd data is in-sample for the Math-Shepherd PRM and shares problems with RLHFlow-Mistral. Qwen PRM training queries are unpublished, so their overlap is UNVERIFIED.
4. Main comparability risk: competitors use non-official subsets and metrics: CROP's 5,000 PRMBench traces with an unstated selection rule and pooled AUROC with supervised splits; SLIDER's redundancy-only accuracy; ARES's subset with 5-fold CV. Matched numbers need re-running their protocol, not copying tables. ProcessBench and LSR-Ben label only the first error.
5. License and status risks:
   - Hard2Verify and CRV are CC-BY-NC (Hard2Verify is also encrypted, with a no-competing-products clause).
   - DeltaBench, Socratic-PRMBench and Math-Shepherd state no license.
   - The venues of Socratic-PRMBench, MedPRMBench, LSR-Ben, CROP, PRISM and VerifySteer are UNVERIFIED.
   - PRMBench-STEM and LSR-Ben have no public data.
