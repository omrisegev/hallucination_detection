01

The input correction matters

Teacher forcing scores a supplied answer; it generates no new answer. The old prefix omitted the empty think block expected along the selected Qwen3 no-think path. The campaign added it, then separately masked token 0. Those are two interventions: the three-stage chart keeps their effects distinct.

1 · Supplied answerQuestion + corrected assistant prefix
2 · One forward passToken probabilities / written-token surprise
3 · Mask token 0Shift telemetry fields and step spans together
4 · Step channelsFeature bank, answer normalization
5 · Fit & rankOne cell; no labels in label-free fitting
6 · Decide flagsSeparate threshold or quota rule

MEAN / ga falls from 0.791 to 0.777 on N16. The correction weakens the headline score; it does not erase the signal.

Independent pairwise AUC from saved corrected scores, exact IDs and offsets, finite columns, answer normalization, masking edge cases and a 1,000-draw within-answer label null. 02

More channels do not automatically improve fusion

The eight label-free scorers are close on N16: 0.771–0.780. A filtered equal average reaches the same region as the more elaborate methods. Across larger banks, LR improves while label-free curves flatten or fall. This is evidence of supervised headroom; the curve alone does not identify its cause.

N13 → N16: make the digit contribution visible

The mean across eight methods rises from 0.764 to 0.778 when adding the digit channels in this pair. This is a descriptive development comparison. It needs a paired, source-group interval before a significance claim.

Digit-inclusive banks are allowed by the later September 30 decision. Report both N13 and N16; do not hide the dependency or reinstate the older prohibition.

03

The strongest claim is specific to error type

A fixed MEAN / ga scorer performs well on redundancy and circularity, while the PRM is stronger on several fluent but invalid statements. Keep one method fixed across the chart; choosing a different winner in each error type would overstate a deployable result.

What PRMBench calls an error

Redundant and circular steps may preserve the final answer’s correctness. They test efficiency and logical simplicity. The benchmark modifies correct solutions into typed cases, then applies quality checks. This is a broader target than detecting arithmetic mistakes. PRMBench paper, §§3–4.

What remains a hypothesis

Repeated text, step position or length may explain part of the advantage. The paper describes general PRM weaknesses here; it does not establish that this dataset was targeted against our particular PRM comparator. Validate the advantage after controlling those cues.

Candidate research claim: one label-free pass provides complementary simplicity-error signal. The current evidence does not establish superiority across all error types.

04

Ranking and clean-answer detection tell different stories

The N16 MEAN / ga Q80 score is 0.6523. I reproduced it both independently and with the upstream evaluator’s numerical functions on the exported slice. Synthetic correct controls are excluded from this pooled score. Their false flags therefore need a separate panel.

Category naming needs correction. The original page calls the two-subtype Sensitivity mean “official,” while omitting multi-solution consistency. Multi-solutions have no error-versus-correct AUC, but their valid-step judgments still matter. This page labels that mean explicitly and does not claim full leaderboard parity.

For the 160 multi-solution answers, upstream numerical parity gives valid-step F1 = 0.8854 for MEAN / ga Q80. Applying the same half-F1 formula yields 0.4427 because error F1 is zero. Including it in a three-subtype arithmetic mean gives 0.5466; this is a declared reconstruction, not a certified leaderboard aggregation. The complete official reporting contract needs to be pinned.

Threshold, quota, and population contract

R0: answer-normalize scores, then take one global 80th percentile over all scored steps. It does not guarantee 20% of every answer. R1: one bank-wide DS-survivor mean, globally standardized; not a replay of the earlier decision-rule implementation. R2: take each answer’s R1 flag count and choose steps by the method ranking. TOP1/TOP2: forced counts; TOP2 is benchmark-informed and remains diagnostic.

Scorers fit pooled cells, not isolated answers. No labels in the fitted label-free formula does not mean bank selection, feature design, quotas and presentation choices were held out. This evaluation is transductive development evidence.

Export: 6,969 answers / 94,203 steps. Within-AUC: 6,030 mixed-label answers; top-one error hit: 6,035 erroneous answers. Noncontrol pooled F1: 6,211 answers; synthetic controls: 758; all actual error-free answers: 934. Five raw duplicate rows are dropped by the loader. One hundred rows contain out-of-range error annotations; these stay inert when matching existing steps.

External rescoring is done; confirmation is not

The linked page says external benchmarks have not been rescored. The current branch contains them. Two protocols answer different questions, so keep them separate:

Locked bundle: target telemetry is corrected, but the source-trained L-SML bundle remains fitted on pre-fix data because its source inputs are gone. Monday panel: label-free fitting is repeated on each corrected target cell. The latter is exploratory target adaptation, not frozen source-only transfer. QwQ was not rerun.

False equivalence claim

The saved BASE interval [−0.021, +0.052] extends beyond the +0.03 margin. The evaluator tests a small point estimate plus an interval crossing zero. This is not equivalence. A numerical counterexample returns “equivalent” even for CI [−1,+1].

Evaluator

Action: Withdraw equivalence wording; use a predeclared TOST or an interval fully inside the margin.

Held-out labels enter restricted LR selection

R1/R2/R3 feature sets are selected from mean coefficients across all five outer models, then evaluated with nested_lr. Other outer models trained on each held-out fold. Full LR reference is a separate result and is not affected.

Selection

Action: Keep these as supervised diagnostics, or repeat selection within each outer training fold.
