A high pooled AUC is not automatically better localization. Risk-IU raises pooled AUC from 0.68131 to 0.75835, while within-answer AUC changes from 0.76881 to 0.77892 with an improvement interval including zero. Applying the sampled answer scale to the original IU ranking already gives pooled AUC 0.74876. This is a post-evaluation diagnostic, not a new candidate or a causal decomposition.

The strong simple control's PB improvement over its full-grid version has a 95% exploratory interval of −3.29 to +8.03 percentage points. GLS versus IU has intervals of −0.0261 to +0.0288 AUROC and −8.76 to +9.33 PB points. Neither establishes a reliable winner. A permuted graph result cannot demonstrate useful graph alignment or learned Joint superiority.

The important repairs and limits

  • Label repair: the PRMB writer used a one-based error-step number as a zero-based index. Codex inherited these derived targets. Earlier reviews compared derived arrays and missed the raw annotation contract. All 6,969 rows were checked and repaired; 6,035 target arrays changed. PB labels and scores were unchanged.
  • Reproducibility: frozen protocols, source/output hashes, explicit fallback coverage, common-ID comparisons and source-group bootstrap intervals were added or extended. Historical rescoring preserves original predictions rather than calling a benchmark correction an algorithm improvement.
  • Latest review: 110 raw label/span joins, 16,390 inherited row records, 880 independent noise/GLS reconstructions, 990 direct vector-IMM replays, 2,970 trajectory/step/GMM checks, 176 metric bundles, 50 paired comparisons and five explicit 1,000-draw bootstraps passed. This is same-session code/mathematical review, not external review or a new model-forward audit.
  • Still open: actual LOCA, Diverging Flows and KalmanNet adaptations; sparse/short-error sampling; full comparator coverage; corrected multi-answer refits; untouched confirmation; historical 24-cell final-answer transfer. No publishable superiority claim yet.

The next bounded decision should use the separate peak and no-error evidence. The tested IMM level readout lost 13 correct PB decisions and gained four relative to its static hold control. GLS and the strong simple control stay in the comparison; a small point lead does not justify a broad parameter search.
