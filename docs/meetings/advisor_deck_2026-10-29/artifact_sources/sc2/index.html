Main metrics: PRMBench PRMScore; Socratic, CRV-Arith, CRV-GSM8K, PRM800K and DeltaBench step macro-F1; Hard2Verify official step balanced F1 (177 answers); ProcessBench average first-error F1. Every SUB5 and TELE/CHAIN/MEAN number uses the label-free GAP count rule; on ProcessBench the NC rule (the PRM's native gate and count, the arm's order). "The PRM itself" is that PRM under the same rule (ProcessBench: its native threshold).

Published scores: PRMBench, Socratic and Hard2Verify leaderboards; ProcessBench from the Qwen PRM paper (Table 7) and R-PRM; CROP Table 6 for kept % and AUROC (its Direct PRM and Trace+PRM use Qwen2.5-Math-PRM-7B, so they are compared with that PRM only). PRM-specific published rows count only against the same PRM. DeltaBench has no published number on our metric. LLM critics and CROP's supervised rows use labels or many model calls that SUB5 does not.

Two cells have no SUB5 score because the six channels form fewer than three L-SML groups: CRV-Arith backbone Qwen-72B (SUB5 + PRM) and CRV-Arith standalone Qwen-7B (SUB5 alone).
