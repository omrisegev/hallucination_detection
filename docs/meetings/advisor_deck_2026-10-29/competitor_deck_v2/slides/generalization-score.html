Same picture in PRMScore, on every benchmark

PRMScore points above the PRM, same GAP rule for all three.

Out of sample, AUTO beats the PRM in 7 of 24 cells and TELE16 in 6, mostly with RLHFlow. Against the strong PRMs: at most 2 of 18. Head to head: AUTO 13, TELE16 11.

On CRV the PRM’s own 0.5 threshold is far better than GAP (e.g. 77 vs 52 PRMScore), so there GAP itself is the bottleneck.

PRMScore = 0.5·F1(correct steps) + 0.5·F1(wrong steps), over all steps. It is the official metric of PRMBench and the main metric of Socratic, DeltaBench and CRV; for Hard2Verify and ProcessBench (steps up to the first error) we computed it in the same form. Sources: bracha_lr_transfer_v1, generalization_prmscore_form_v1.
