SLIDER: telemetry finds redundant steps best

Plain accuracy, as SLIDER reports: TELE16 76%, AUTO 76%, Step-RRI 62%.

Same protocol for every arm: a 1-D LR on the score sets the threshold on a labelled half, with class-balanced weights.

Why balanced weights: 87% of steps are not redundant. An unweighted threshold never flags anything and scores 86.8% (PRM 86.8, AUTO 88.2, TELE16 89.1). SLIDER’s 62% implies they did not do that.

PRMBench redundancy class, 758 answers, Qwen-PRM-7B pairing; 10 splits 50/50 by answer. Balanced accuracy gives the same picture: PRM 60.4, AUTO 75.6, TELE16 76.0. With our label-free GAP rule instead of the LR threshold, balanced accuracy is TELE16 74.7, AUTO 72.3, PRM 54.7. Other pairings: TELE16 76–79, AUTO 69–76. SLIDER’s split is not stated and no code is released.
