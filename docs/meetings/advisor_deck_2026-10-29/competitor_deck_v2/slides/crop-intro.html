CROP: certify the longest error-free start of a solution

What it does

Scores each step’s risk, then a conformal threshold keeps the longest prefix with at most 5% risk of holding an error

Risk scores

Random, text format, Llama-8B telemetry LR, Qwen-PRM-7B, LR on telemetry + PRM

Labels

Yes: LRs trained on 60% of each dataset; every threshold calibrated on 20%

Benchmarks

6, incl. PRMBench, ProcessBench. Metrics: pooled step AUROC, % kept. Code released

How we run it

CROP’s own protocol for every arm, GAP not used: split 60/20/20; on the 20%, pick with labels the highest threshold so that ≤5% of kept prefixes hold an error; on test, keep each answer up to its first step above it. Only the score is ours

“Kept” = average share of each answer’s steps that is certified, at risk ≤ 5%.

CROP's Trace+PRM LR is close to Bracha's suggestion: a supervised LR on PRM + telemetry. The difference: CROP trains on labels from the same benchmark. Their Table 17 shows calibration does not transfer across datasets (14.8% risk against a 5% target).
