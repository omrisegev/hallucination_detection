The paper · arXiv 2507.12948, EMNLP 2025

ARES: check each step against the steps already verified

What it does

Scores each step by the probability that an LLM finds it entailed by sampled sets of earlier sound steps

Cost

About 7 to 100 LLM calls per step (GPT-4o-mini or Qwen3-4B)

Labels

Score is training-free; the threshold is tuned by 5-fold CV on labels

Benchmarks

PRMBench (subset, size not stated), DeltaBench, 2 synthetic. Metric: Macro-F1

Code

Released. We applied its threshold protocol to our scores

Headline: PRMBench Macro-F1 64.0. In its own appendix, the Qwen PRM alone (74.9) beats ARES (73.6).

We did not rerun ARES's entailment scoring, which needs many LLM calls per step. We used its decision protocol, one global threshold per method chosen by 5-fold CV on labels, folds grouped by source problem, on all of PRMBench.
