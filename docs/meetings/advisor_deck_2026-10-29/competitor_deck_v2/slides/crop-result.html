Pooled step AUROC ranks all steps of all answers together, so scores must be comparable across answers. Our usual within-answer AUC ranks steps only inside each answer, then averages. Our per-answer standardization removes each answer’s level: that lowers pooled AUROC and hurts CROP’s single global threshold.
