PRISM: retrain the PRM so it passes fewer wrong steps

What it does

Retrains Qwen-PRM-7B with a pairwise loss on correct vs wrong steps, with hard negatives and a curriculum

Labels

Yes: about 220k step pairs from PRM800K

Benchmark

PRMBench (FPR, TPR, PRMScore), ProcessBench

Headline

FPR 69.3 → 47.1 at TPR 87.3; PRMScore 65.5 → 68.0

Code

Repository is empty; no checkpoint

Positive class = correct step. FPR = share of wrong steps passed as correct.

PRISM claims that standard PRMs over-credit plausible but wrong steps. Our PRM row reproduces their baseline exactly (65.49). Moving the untouched PRM's threshold to PRISM's TPR already gives FPR 47.7 and PRMScore 68.3.
