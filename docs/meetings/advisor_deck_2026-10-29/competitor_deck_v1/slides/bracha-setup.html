Bracha’s experiment: a supervised LR in place of L-SML

Training

One LR per PRM on PRM800K’s human step labels. L2 penalty, balanced classes, C chosen by grouped CV.

Inputs

Exactly AUTO’s 17: the PRM plus the 16 backbone channels. Controls: the PRM alone, and standalone telemetry alone.

Leakage control

823 of 5,049 problems that also appear in the test sets were removed. Keeping them moves the result by at most 1.9 points.

Testing

Applied unchanged to 7 benchmarks under AUTO’s rule. Protocol frozen before any result; 92 parity checks pass.

Source: results/bracha_lr_transfer_v1/PROTOCOL.json and SUMMARY.md

Caveat to say up front: PRM800K is the closest public step-labelled set. It is not the Qwen PRMs' own training data, which is not public. PRISM was trained on PRM800K.
