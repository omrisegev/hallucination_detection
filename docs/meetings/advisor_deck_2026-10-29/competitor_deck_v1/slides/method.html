CHAIN

The PRM is forced into its own group. This is prior knowledge, so it is shown only for reference.

Every arm uses the same rule: per answer, flag the steps above the largest gap in the sorted scores. On ProcessBench we use its first-error rule.

4 PRMs: Qwen-PRM-7B, Qwen-PRM-72B, PURE, RLHFlow. Math-Shepherd is left out.

Why AUTO and not CHAIN: CHAIN tells the algorithm that the PRM is special. That breaks the no-prior-knowledge principle. In AUTO the data merged the PRM into a telemetry group in 32 of 32 cases.
