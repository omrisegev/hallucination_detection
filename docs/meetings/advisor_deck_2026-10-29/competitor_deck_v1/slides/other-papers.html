Three more papers: why no direct number

R-PRM · EMNLP 2025

Generative PRM trained on PRM800K, 10 critiques per step. PRMBench 66.8, ProcessBench 70.4.

Compared on slide 4. AUTO is 3.1 higher. The paper does not define its metric as PRMScore.

Mind the Gap · ICML 2026

One telemetry channel: drops in top-20 entropy. GSM8K and MATH answers, and exact first-error match on ProcessBench.

No shared metric: no PRMBench, not ProcessBench’s F1. Its code covers only final answers. We can add it as a 17th channel.

BR2-PRM · ACL 2026

Supervised naive Bayes over 9 error-type scores from an MLLM judge, on its own multimodal PRMBench-V.

No shared benchmark: our text PRMs cannot read images, and no code is released.

Sources: competitor_primer_2026-10-22/MTG_BR2.md, competitor_explainers_he_2026-10-24/09_RPRM.md

These were in the email's code table. R-PRM is now on the PRMBench chart. Mind the Gap is the closest in spirit (label-free telemetry from one pass), so the useful test is to add its drop signal as a channel, not to compare tables.
