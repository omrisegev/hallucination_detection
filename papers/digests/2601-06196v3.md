## Summary

GA-ICL is a supervised, white-box demonstration retriever for a downstream ICL
hallucination classifier. It averages final-layer hidden states, trains a projection
network and class prototypes, regularizes local PCA patches, and selects two global
demonstrations nearest the learned prototypes. Despite the title, it is neither a
sample-specific expert router nor a detector built from unlabeled manifold geometry.

The correctness semantics come from labels and Proxy Anchor metric learning. The paper
does not isolate the marginal value of its manifold loss from a capacity-matched
supervised bottleneck/prototype learner.

## Geometry, supervision, and access contract

- **Geometric object:** mean-pooled final-layer hidden states of the full evidence,
  question, and candidate-answer text.
- **Actual geometry:** local PCA patches are grown around anchors while reconstruction
  quality remains above a threshold. Orthogonal and within-patch distances feed a
  manifold loss. This is real local-linear machinery, but embedded in supervised
  prototype learning.
- **Supervision:** explicit hallucination/factuality labels train the projection head
  and two class proxies. The authors call the method training-light, not training-free.
- **Inference:** an additional two-shot ICL judge pass over the completed candidate;
  the same selected demonstrations are reused globally rather than routed per query.
- **Access:** white-box hidden states plus labelled demonstrations.
- **Tasks:** completed-response Global classification only; not prefix-causal and no
  first-error output.

## Datasets & models used

- FEVER with its official training/test split.
- HaluEval QA, dialogue, and summarization; HaluEval has no official split in this use.
- Main models: Qwen3-4B, Llama3-8B, Falcon3-3B, GPT-Neo-2.7B, Mistral-7B, Vicuna-7B.
- Scale extension: Phi-14B and Qwen3-32B.

## Methods it compared itself against

KNN, clustering, BM25, perplexity retrieval, SA-ICL, AdaICL, and Top-k+Con-E under
the same two-shot ICL budget. A small LoRA-SFT resource comparison is also reported.
Missing controls are more important for our use: proxy-anchor-only, bottleneck-only,
manifold-only, random geometry, and a capacity-matched supervised classifier.

## Experiments — methodology & scores

All primary outputs use temperature zero and report one deterministic accuracy value,
without CIs. The reported tables use an outcome-optimized prototype dimension.

| Model / task | GA-ICL accuracy | Strong comparison | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Qwen3-4B / HaluEval Dialogue | 72.0 | 70.0 | Small gain |
| Qwen3-4B / HaluEval QA | 70.5 | Perplexity 74.0 | GA-ICL loses |
| Mistral-7B / Dialogue | 69.6 | KNN 59.7 | Largest cited gain, +9.9 points |
| Mistral-7B / QA | 62.0 | BM25 66.0 | Lexical evidence wins |
| Phi-14B / Dialogue, Summ., QA | 75.9 / 65.4 / 81.2 | — | Scale extension |
| Qwen3-32B / Dialogue, Summ., QA | 83.5 / 67.3 / 71.7 | — | Scale extension |

Training is 200 epochs on an A6000 and costs about 3–4 hours and 640 MB per
dataset-model pair. Performance generally saturates near ten demonstrations.

## Calibration, leakage, and failure cases

- FEVER uses train rows for retrieval and held-out test rows for evaluation.
- For HaluEval, demonstrations are removed before evaluation, but the paper does not
  establish that the projection network/proxies were fit without labels from the
  remaining evaluation rows. A target firewall cannot be verified.
- Prototype dimensionality is chosen through accuracy ablation with no described nested
  validation. The default reportedly gives 53.0, showing that tuning matters greatly.
- The method is weaker where explicit evidence makes BM25 highly informative.
- The authors acknowledge that query-aware prototype selection is future work; the
  current method is not a per-sample router.
- No code repository was found in the extract; FEVER and HaluEval are public.

## Connection to our pipeline

GA-ICL adds three resources unavailable to current IU-PCR: correctness labels, full
hidden states, and an extra labelled-ICL judge pass. Applying only its local PCA loss
to atomic or DSP features would return to the same \(P(X)\) that failed our routers.
Its results therefore do not show that unlabeled feature geometry identifies family
reliability.

As a comparator it belongs in a separate supervised white-box/critic tier. For Global
IU research, balanced global LR or the supervised atomic ceiling is a cheaper and fairer
control. It has no direct Localization or Early applicability.

## Recommendation

**Reject as an IU/router candidate.** Retain only as a trained white-box ICL baseline
and as evidence that target-supervised bottlenecks can organize hidden-state geometry.
