## Summary

PCNET projects the final-layer residual-stream state through a learned MLP and fits a
Neural Probabilistic Circuit whose score gates a decoding-time correction mechanism,
PC-LDCD. The paper describes high score as departure from a “factual manifold,” but
the projector and circuit are jointly trained with factual/hallucinated labels and a
contrastive margin. It is therefore a supervised density classifier, not target discovery
from unlabeled latent geometry.

PCNET does add a real information channel unavailable to current IU-PCR—white-box
residual states, including counterfactual next-token states for correction. That makes it
potentially useful as a separate supervised white-box baseline, not a gray-box IU repair.

## Geometry, supervision, and access contract

- **Geometric object:** last-valid-token residual-stream states from the final transformer
  block, compressed from roughly 4,096 to 128 dimensions.
- **Actual model:** a learned probabilistic-circuit DAG with Gaussian/Laplace/Student-t
  mixture leaves. This is density estimation, not tangent or ridge estimation.
- **Supervision:** 250 factual and 250 hallucinated samples per setup; the MLP and PC
  jointly minimize factual NLL plus a labelled contrastive margin.
- **Calibration:** the online threshold is selected from the labelled precision-recall
  curve to maximize F1.
- **Access:** full white-box residual stream. PC-LDCD additionally evaluates hypothetical
  next states for the top eight tokens.
- **Sampling:** no repeated generations for detection; correction adds token lookahead.
- **Causality:** the per-token gate uses the current prefix state, but the paper does not
  provide a valid prefix-label or onset evaluation.

## Datasets & models used

- Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct, Qwen3-4B, Mistral-7B-v0.3, and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct.
- CoQA, SQuAD v2.0, TriviaQA, and TruthfulQA.
- Base models are frozen in 4-bit NF4; PCNET is trained on one A100-40GB.
- Main training uses 500 balanced samples, 50 epochs, and three seeds.

## Methods it compared itself against

Detection: Token-NLL, Semantic Entropy Probes, HaloScope, and AutoFact. Correction:
DoLa, ITI, AdaSteer, SADI, ICD, vanilla decoding, and a small RAG comparison.

The paper does not include the decisive capacity controls for our question: the same
MLP with balanced logistic loss, a discriminative MLP with identical parameter count,
or a density model trained without hallucination labels.

## Experiments — methodology & scores

| Model | CoQA / SQuAD / TriviaQA / TruthfulQA AUROC | Mean AUROC |
|---|---|---:|
| Llama-3.2-1B | .95 / .98 / .91 / .66 | .88 |
| Mistral-7B | .97 / .98 / .98 / .79 | .92 |
| Qwen3-4B | .96 / .97 / .95 / .81 | .92 |
| Llama-3.1-8B | .98 / .98 / .97 / .75 | .92 |

PC-LDCD reports a 53.7% mean corruption rate and 79.3% preservation rate.
The corruption rate is still high, and SADI has the same corruption point estimate.
TruthfulQA True+Info is .78 on Qwen3-4B and .72 on Mistral-7B; the Llama-8B
result ties ICD. In the RAG comparison, PC-LDCD improves TruthfulQA MC metrics but
loses TriviaQA exact match to RAG (.290 versus .465).

The data-size ablation nearly saturates CoQA at 100 examples but improves TruthfulQA
until roughly 750. Projection dimensions 32–512 produce mean AUROC around .78–.86
without a monotone trend; dimension 128 is selected using an alignment diagnostic.

## Calibration, leakage, and failure cases

- Twenty-five percent of QA pairs are held for testing, but the paper does not specify
  how sequence labels are mapped to token states or whether all prefixes of a wrong
  answer inherit the final label.
- It does not state whether states from one response are grouped through all splits.
  This prevents a causal Early or Localization interpretation.
- “Exact NLL” means exact evaluation of the fitted circuit score. The modified leaves
  include a learned multiplicative gate and the root is also called “unnormalized”; the
  extract does not establish that the modified circuit remains a normalized density.
- First-token detection and step localization appear only as future work.
- The paper reports an anonymous code URL but no released calibration/hidden-state data.
- Total reported experimental allocation is very large: 10,368 GPU-hour upper bound,
  about 4,147 GPU-hours estimated effective compute.

## Connection to our pipeline

If PCNET were applied to our mixed-v2 or DSP matrix, its labels would be doing the
target identification; without them it would be another flexible density estimator over
the same \(P(X)\). A fair gray-box control is balanced global LR or the supervised atomic
ceiling. A faithful PCNET comparison belongs to a separate supervised white-box access
tier and must not be presented as an IU-PCR variant.

For Early, its causal gate is an interesting architecture hypothesis, but the paper lacks
budgeted AUROC/AUPRC, suffix invariance, first-warning calibration, or onset delay. For
Localization it supplies no evidence.

## Recommendation

**Reject for the current gray-box, label-free fusion program.** Retain as a supervised
white-box comparator and as evidence that new residual-state telemetry can carry target
signal if the project deliberately opens that access tier.
