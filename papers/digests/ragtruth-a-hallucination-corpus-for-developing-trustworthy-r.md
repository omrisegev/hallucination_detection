## Summary

Introduces RAGTruth, a large-scale corpus of nearly 18,000 RAG responses generated by diverse LLMs with fine-grained word-level and span-level human hallucination annotations across summarization, QA, and data-to-text tasks.

## Datasets & models used

18,000 human-annotated RAG outputs across news summarization, QA, and data-to-text generation.

## Methods it compared itself against

Automated RAG evaluation judges and existing hallucination benchmarks.

## Experiments — methodology & scores

Evaluates word-level and span-level hallucination detection across RAG generation tasks.

| Setup | Task Domain | Annotation Scale | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| RAG Responses | QA / Summarization / Data-to-Text | ~18,000 annotated outputs | Fine-grained span-level factual conflict labeling |

## Connection to our pipeline

Key reference dataset for validating token-level error localization and unsupervised detectors in RAG pipelines.

## Notes / open questions

Provides word-level and span-level ground truth annotations for RAG hallucinations.
