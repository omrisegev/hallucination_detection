What the three methods change

Deployed U-PCR solves the original pairwise covariance equations and may exclude weak features. IU-PCR keeps the full pool and uses two covariance components. DUFS-LIU-PCR first learns label-free continuous feature gates, builds a k=7 sample graph, and adds the frozen Laplacian penalty λ=0.1. The task adapter changes the prediction unit; the solvers do not change.
