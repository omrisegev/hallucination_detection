Before/exact/after/invalid partition all erroneous answers by their raw peak. Suppressed means a correct raw peak was followed by a no-error decision. Final exact counts include the gate. First-error versus prefix AUC compares the first erroneous step only with its earlier correct steps, then averages over supported erroneous answers with a nonempty prefix. Step-zero errors are excluded explicitly. Later steps have no added correctness labels. This conditional diagnostic differs from exact first-error/no-error accuracy and from PRMB's per-step ranking.

The five dual methods fit one answer alone; Joint graphs here use the original condition1000. Historical IU, fixed-family CONT and equal_all23 fit other training answers and use nested PB label calibration. This analysis reloads the actual historical PB top-10 readout before checking peaks or prefix rankings.
