What changed

Covariance defined a six-dimensional subspace. Third moments separated up to five latent components. The component closest to IU-PCR's label-free reliability vector was retained; other components were removed before rerunning the unchanged IU-PCR and DUFS-LIU solvers.

This is an exploratory continuous adaptation. It is not the categorical tensor algorithm or theorem from Ibrahim et al. (2025).
