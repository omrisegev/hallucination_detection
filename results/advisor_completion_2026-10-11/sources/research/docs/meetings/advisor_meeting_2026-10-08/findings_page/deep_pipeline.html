Tail Pipeline Deep Dive

The binary tail-vote pipeline of the last three weeks, component by component, and why fusion saturates. Collected from the full slide deck; figures are light-theme images.

2. Tail-vote pipeline: no gain; the loss is inside groups

Three weeks: tail votes, a Dawid–Skene filter, L-SML groups and merge, mean / HEM / TCI inside groups, MoM between groups.

Do binary votes beat continuous fusion?

No. Same banks, PRMBench: vs IU-PCR, clearly better in 9, clearly worse in 21 (of 40). Vs continuous L-SML: 11 / 26.

tails · terms · tailsq · baselines

Do the L-SML groups help?

Not yet shown. As input to group weights, random groups of the same sizes do as well (0.787 vs 0.784 AUC). The merge matters only on N13 and N16.

merge · withinloss

Where is the loss?

Inside groups. MoM group weights are near the supervised optimum (0.782 vs 0.784). Equal averaging inside groups loses: positive supervised weights recover 86–97% of the gap.

between · mom · estacc · withinloss

What failed?

Every label-free rule inside groups: HEM weights, DS top-3 pruning, TCI triplets. None gives a stable PRMBench gain across banks; on ProcessBench all score lower.

filter · hem · tci · pipeline

Net: vs the DS-signed average, the pipeline ties on PRMBench (15 better, 15 worse of 36) and loses on ProcessBench (3 better, 9 worse).

3. Pipeline: votes, filter, groups, group weights

1 · Read

Teacher-forced token probabilities of the given answer

2 · Channels

11 to 56 step features (one nested bank)

3 · Rescale

z-score each channel inside its own answer

4 · Vote

Each channel marks its top 20% steps as "error"

5 · Filter

Drop channels estimated worse than chance

6 · Group

Cluster channels that move together; merge near-duplicate groups

7 · Inside groups

Plain mean, learned weights (HEM) or a triplet (TCI)

8 · Between groups

Weight each group by its estimated accuracy (MoM)

9 · Score

Weighted sum of the group scores

10 · Decide

Which steps to flag, and whether the answer is clean

Four methods we compare differ in steps 4 and 7: votes per channel or per group, and a plain mean, HEM or TCI inside groups. All use MoM between groups (step 8).

Controls: the plain average stops after step 3. The DS-signed average flips wrong-way channels (Dawid–Skene, as in step 5) instead of dropping them. The filtered average stops after step 5.

Source: Pipeline · DS-signed control: monday_ds_signed_v1/PROTOCOL.json

4. Tail votes: each channel flags its top 20% of steps

The rule

In each answer, channel i votes f_i = +1 ("error") on its top k = max(1, ⌈0.2·n⌉) steps, and −1 on the rest. n = steps in the answer.

Because of rounding up (k = ⌈0.2·n⌉), 23% of steps are marked, not 20%.

Why votes

Dawid–Skene (DS), method of moments (MoM) and the spectral meta-learner (SML) are defined for ±1 votes. Each channel acts as a yes/no classifier. We still average the continuous values.

Source: Binary tails · source: monday_ablation_v1 figures/T0_tail_votes_example; ALGORITHM_CARDS.md §1

Speaker notes (Hebrew)
פירוט לשאלות. המסר: כל channel הופך למצביע כן/לא על 20% הצעדים העליונים שלו. הכלל: בכל תשובה channel i מצביע f_i = +1, "שגיאה", על k = max(1, ⌈0.2·n⌉) הצעדים העליונים ו-−1 על השאר, כש-n מספר הצעדים. בגלל העיגול למעלה מסומנים 23% מהצעדים ולא 20% - 0.230 הוא הממוצע על ה-nested banks (ALGORITHM_CARDS.md, ESTIMATOR_VS_TRUTH.csv). למה הצבעות: Dawid–Skene, MoM ו-SML מוגדרים להצבעות ±1, וכל channel מתפקד כ-classifier כן/לא. באיור: תשובה אמיתית מ-PRMBench, 10 צעדים, אז כל channel מסמן 2; השגיאה בצעד 5, המוצלל. entropy מפספס אותה, וה-surprisal של ה-written token והעלייה בו מצביעים עליה. ההצבעות מזינות רק את ה-estimators - filter, partition, דיוקי קבוצות; ציון הצעד הוא סכום משוקלל של ה-z-scores הרציפים בתוך התשובה. שוויונות נשברים במפתח אקראי קבוע. גם תשובות נקיות מצביעות, אז הצבעות שגויות קיימות מעצם ההגדרה. ראיות קודמות: תלות נמוכה יותר בין קבוצות על הצבעות - class-conditional |r| 0.08–0.21 מול 0.14–0.31 ברציף; ומשקלים שנלמדו על הצבעות מפסידים למשקלים שווים, −0.0022 AUC, ולכן ההצבעות נשארות קלט ל-estimators בלבד. דאטה פיתוח.

5. Voter terms: four numbers rate a channel as a voter

One channel, all steps

Votes "error"Votes "correct"
True errorcaught → ψmissed
True correctfalse alarmpassed → η

ψ = caught ÷ error steps; η = passed ÷ correct steps.

A vote = the channel's yes/no mark on a step (previous slide).

  • Error rate (prevalence): share of steps that are wrong. PRMBench: 0.140.
  • Sensitivity ψ: share of wrong steps the channel votes "error".
  • Specificity η: share of correct steps it votes "correct".
  • Balanced accuracy π = (ψ + η)/2. 0.5 = chance. Below 0.5 = the channel points the wrong way.
  • Reliability ranking: the channels ordered by π. Used by SML, which gives an order only.

Filter: keep a channel if its estimated π is above 0.5. Between groups: weights come from the estimated ψ and η.

Check: true values need step labels. PRMBench has them, so we compare estimates with the truth there.

Source: Terms · source: monday_nested_banks_v1 ALGORITHM_CARDS.md (PRMBench, 94,203 steps, true error rate 0.140)

Speaker notes (Hebrew)
פירוט לשאלות. המסר: ארבעה מספרים מתארים כמה channel טוב כמצביע; המטרה היא לתת אוצר מילים לפני כל estimator. הצבעה f_i היא סימון הזנב מהשקף הקודם: +1 אומר "הצעד נראה כמו שגיאה", −1 אחרת. Error rate, או prevalence, הוא חלק הצעדים השגויים - ב-PRMBench 0.140. Sensitivity ψ היא חלק הצעדים השגויים שה-channel מסמן "שגיאה"; specificity η היא חלק הצעדים הנכונים שהוא מסמן "נכון"; π = (ψ + η)/2 היא balanced accuracy, ו-0.5 זה chance. ה-reliability ranking הוא סדר ה-channels לפי π, וזה מה ש-SML נותן. ψ, η ו-π מתארים channel אחד או קבוצה של channels. ה-estimators ה-label-free, Dawid–Skene, method of moments ו-SML, מעריכים אותם בלי labels; SML מחזיר רק סדר. π מתחת ל-0.5 אומר שה-channel מצביע "שגיאה" יותר על צעדים נכונים - הוא anti-oriented. ה-filter שומר channel אם π המוערך מעל 0.5, והמשקלים בין קבוצות באים מ-ψ ו-η המוערכים. רק ל-PRMBench יש labels מלאים ברמת הצעד, 94,203 צעדים, prevalence אמיתי 0.1396, ולכן כל בדיקת הערכה מול אמת היא על PRMBench; ProcessBench מתייג רק את השגיאה הראשונה. דאטה פיתוח.

7. Filter: finds wrong-way channels; on big banks, flip them

Dawid–Skene model

P(f) = Σ_y P(y) · Π_i P(f_i | y)

f_i = vote of channel i at a step. y = the true class of the step, unknown. The model assumes channels are independent once y is known.

Expectation–maximisation (EM), an iterative fit with 5 starts, estimates ψ_i and η_i. We keep channel i if π̂_i = (ψ̂_i + η̂_i)/2 > 0.5.

Origin: Dawid & Skene, J. R. Stat. Soc. C, 1979. The keep rule is ours.

Vs truth (8 older September banks): error rate 0.313 vs 0.140; channel order Spearman 0.741.

HEM and TCI work inside groups. They are not channel filters, so we did not test them here.

  • 5 nested banks N13–N56. MoM and SML filters drop the same channels as DS on N13–N25; within ±0.002 AUC on N43–N56.
  • No filter: −0.008 (N43), −0.004 (N56); at most +0.002 on smaller banks.
  • Keep all, flip sign (post hoc, not adopted): +0.014 to +0.026 on the 3 large banks, −0.006 on N13 and N16.
  • ProcessBench hit: every change within ±0.008.

8. Merge step: it matters only on N13 and N16

How groups form

L-SML clusters channels whose votes depend on each other beyond the truth. Then we merge. Never fewer than 3 groups.

Jaffe et al. 2016; merge rule ours.

Merge rule ρ

ρ = λ₂(R[A∪B]) ÷ min(λ₁(R[A]), λ₁(R[B])). R = vote correlations; λ₁, λ₂ = first and second eigenvalue.

Small ρ: A and B act as one factor. Merge while ρ < 0.5.

How badly do we need it?

Only on small nested banks, where it joins two halves of the level family. PRMBench: N13 4→3 groups, N16 5→4; N25, N43, N56 keep 6, 7, 8 groups with no merge.

Without it, the main recipe (mean inside groups, MoM weights between) loses 0.008 AUC on N13 and N16.

10. MoM: good channel order; its error rate echoes the quota

Model, symbol by symbol

  • f_i ∈ {−1, +1}: vote of channel i at a step (+1 = "error").
  • t_i: how strongly channel i tracks the truth. 0 = random.
  • b = share of error steps minus share of correct steps (0 = balanced).
  • Pairs: E[f_i f_j] = t_i t_j (i ≠ j). Two channels agree only through the truth.
  • Triples: E[f_i f_j f_k] = α t_i t_j t_k, α = −2b / √(1 − b²). Triples add b.
  • From t and b: ψ_i and η_i in closed form. No EM, no local optima.

Origin: Jaffe, Nadler, Kluger, AISTATS 2015 (Algorithm 1). Use: group weights in every recipe that weights groups by MoM.

12. HEM: no gain on PRMBench, one point lost on ProcessBench

Model in words

  • Each group g has a hidden yes/no state a_g: "this group sees an error".
  • The state depends on the true class y. Members vote from the state, not from y.
  • e₁: chance that a member votes "error" when its group state is "error". e₀: the same when the state is "ok".
  • Member weight w_i = max(0, log(e₁(1 − e₀) / (e₀(1 − e₁)))). Groups under 3 members get equal weights.

Origin: the dependent-classifier model of Jaffe et al. 2016. The full-EM estimator is ours; no paper uses it.

Use: HEM sets the weights inside groups. MoM still weights the groups.

13. TCI triplets: they detect dependence; no gain transfers

Prop. 2.4 in words

C_pq = how votes of channels p and q move together (pair moment). T_pqs = the same for three channels (triple moment). If channels are independent given the truth, T_pqs ÷ C_pq is the same number for every pair (p, q).

My triplet rule

  • In each group with 4 or more members, try every triplet plus an anchor (the mean of the other groups).
  • V = mean over s of 1 − cos²(T, C). V = 0 when the two are proportional.
  • Keep the triplet with the smallest V. MoM weights the groups.

Origin: Prop. 2.4 of Lee et al., FUSE, arXiv:2604.18547, 2026 (a diagnostic there). Selection rule: mine, 5 Oct.

What it detects, what it buys

ProcessBench: −0.010 [−0.014, −0.005] (8 original banks).

7 complete nested banks: below the plain-mean recipe on PRMBench in 5 of 7 (0.771 vs 0.775) and on ProcessBench in 6 of 7 (0.369 vs 0.374).

Externally (N16): below the DS-filter average on both Socratic cells.

• המסר: ברמת הצעד ה-fusion מגיע לרוויה, כמו שקרה ברמת התשובה באוגוסט. • אוגוסט: 13 שיטות בטווח 1.4 נקודות, 24 cells, 48,607 תשובות. ממוצע פשוט של שש משפחות היה במקום השני. • עכשיו, N16: 8 כללים label-free בטווח 0.01 AUC (PRMBench 0.787 עד 0.796; ProcessBench SLA 0.396 עד 0.416). ה-sign control הוא עכשיו ה-DS-signed average; ב-N16 הוא זהה לכל תשובה ל-oriented average הקודם, אז המספרים לא השתנו. • הסתייגות: N16 נבחר אחרי שראיתי תוצאות. • banks גדולים: הטווח עד 0.04, ב-N40, בעיקר כי continuous L-SML קורס ל-0.724 (IU-PCR 0.764). בלי L-SML הטווח לכל היותר 0.019. • עם labels: logistic regression על אותם channels מוסיף 0.026 AUC על N16 ו-0.03 עד 0.07 בסך הכול ב-PRMBench, וכמעט כלום ב-ProcessBench. • למה: כלל label-free מוצא אילו channels זזים יחד, אבל לא איזה כיוון של התנועה המשותפת הוא השגיאה. המידע הנוסף שה-labels מנצלים נמצא ב-contrasts בין channels. • המסקנה לתזה: המאמץ צריך ללכת לייצוג ולהחלטה על תשובה נקייה, לא לכללי fusion חדשים.
