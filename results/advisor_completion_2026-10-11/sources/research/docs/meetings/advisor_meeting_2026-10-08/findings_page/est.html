What the label-free estimators get right

The estimators (DS, MoM) rank the channels well: a channel with high true accuracy gets a high estimate (Spearman 0.73–0.80). We use this order to drop wrong-way channels and to weight groups. But they estimate the error rate 1.5–2.2 times too high (0.21–0.31 vs 0.14), because the channels share information beyond the truth. We do not use that rate.
