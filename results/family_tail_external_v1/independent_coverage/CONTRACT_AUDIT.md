## Scope and recipe fidelity

Reviewed the family15-tail20-transfer-v1 handoff, exact transfer lock, portable scorer, 48-channel extraction modules, source parity runner, external runner, and original hist29_align / pool_structure / union_structure source snapshots. The portable scorer and transfer lock are byte-identical to the requested worktree. Lock SHA256: 65b35336fcc2f66b7843ec040d3bdafbbaa03bb44ae5f61f1d00335abfaea5cf.

The 48 channels comprise 11 bank channels, 6 additional CT7 channels (duplicate ve1 is excluded), 4 H1 shape channels, evidence-drop risk, and 26 retained historical channels. The retained historical order exactly omits trace length, raw entropy, and raw spilled energy from the original 29-stream bank. Original source signs are used on A48/K28/family representations; bank11 deliberately uses its own unchanged raw directions. No digit feature is introduced.

Historical token_feature_views.py and feature_utils.py match the project originals at AST level. Their full-answer CUSUM, prefix backfill and interpolated STFT should not be described as causal online features. The hist29 aligner reduces finite float64 token values by Top10. Its raw storage is not rounded to float32. Bank11, conversely, retains the existing float32 token representation. CT7 follows float64 storage and its own float32 intermediate only where the original temporal residual construction requires it. This distinction must be preserved.

The CT7 chosen-standardized stream is read as channel 3 of token_calibration and then Top10-reduced; there is no first-step replacement. H1_frac_above_z uses robust answer normalization (median, IQR/1.349, then standard-deviation/one fallback) and the fraction of step tokens >= 1, not a Top10 mean. H1 first token, slope and jump have their separate exact definitions. Evidence drop uses top20 normalized negative entropy, alpha=1/3 smoothing initialized at the first value, negative first difference, historical float32 storage, then step Top10. Historical missing step values are replaced by the answer-column mean, with a wholly missing column becoming zero; this is explicit source behavior rather than an unreported fallback. Input telemetry completeness is validated first.

The candidate first standardizes channels within answers, applies locked signs, averages members into 15 fixed families, and standardizes families. The frozen tail-trained weights act on continuous family values; target tail marks are not fitted or used for threshold calibration. Final answer standardization uses the locked 1e-8 standard-deviation floor. Each method applies its own frozen source-fold-4 threshold using strict score < threshold for valid-step prediction. The source fit API centers tie-aware top-ceil(20% n) marks without adding an extra pooled z-score. The tail model is not a new EM confidence model and not the alternative strict-quantile binary model.

An independent synthetic matrix reconstruction (14 steps, 3 answers, including a singleton and a constant channel) checked every one of the 9 newly scored arms without calling build_representations or answer_standardize. Maximum score difference was 6.661338147750939e-16; all threshold decisions agreed. A boundary-tie fixture checked fractional one-third mass on three tied maxima and uniform constant-column marks. These are algebra checks, not external performance evidence.
