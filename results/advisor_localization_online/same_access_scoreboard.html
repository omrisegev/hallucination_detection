What these numbers do not say

  • The DeepConf columns are our own approximate proxies, computed from saved log-probabilities — not the published method. Whether the pinned official confidence removes their indistinguishability from us is pre-registered null N7 of the prefix-lane claim registry, and it is exactly what the DeepConf acquisition now running exists to answer.
  • Parity is not a win. Five of the six same-access competitors in early detection, and three of the six in localization, have intervals crossing zero. We lead on the point estimate in both lanes and can claim a clean separation in only one place.
  • The pre-registered verdict of this audit was REGRESSES_DIRECT_COMPETITOR, because the new global-detector architecture scores 0.5882 on early detection against 0.6104 for our existing prefix method. The architecture did not beat what we already had.
  • Potential tokens remaining are not realized savings, and higher-access rows are ceilings rather than competitors.
