Interpretation

The result supports the original hypothesis in a narrow sense: a consensus of signals spread through depth can beat an evaluation-selected single layer, and deployed U-PCR slightly exceeds the standalone TriLens linear probe on macro AUROC. It does not dominate AUPRC: TriLens remains higher there. The AUROC margin is small, and the bootstrap interval determines whether the present data support more than a numerical discovery.
