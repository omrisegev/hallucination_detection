  • topk_tail_mass and renyi_entropy_2 rank #1 and #5 of 30 by individual informativeness, yet neither improves a hand-curated subset. High individual informativeness does not imply additive value — the information is already covered.
  • The honest bar is LOCO_5 (0.7705), not GOOD_6: LOCO_5 already contains topk_tail_mass, picked independently by the Step-195 exhaustive LOCO search. The view is valuable; adding it to a subset that already covers it is not.
  • All six were pre-registered together, so the best is a ceiling.
