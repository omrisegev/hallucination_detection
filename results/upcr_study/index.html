U-PCR: faithful implementation, and a clustered variant

Making the implementation match its paper, then testing whether clustering fixes what dependence breaks — corrected after adversarial review

In short

  • Every claimed deviation from the paper was real; none of them helps.
  • Two previously-settled results reverse on the fixed implementation.
  • The clustered variant's premise was a confound, and the variant fails.
  • The g2 search range binds on the deployed estimate but costs nothing; the component-count threshold is the real dial.
  • The finding that survives is about feature orientation, not U-PCR.

What this study was for, and what happened

Two review documents claimed our U-PCR implementation had drifted from the paper it cites. All seven claimed deviations checked out. The implementation was made faithful — each deviation behind its own flag — and every one measured. Prior U-PCR numbers were re-run rather than carried over, because two of them had been measured with the very bug they were testing.

An independent adversarial review of this study then found 17 defects of its own, several of which changed a stated conclusion. The numbers below are post-correction. Where a claim was withdrawn, it is named rather than quietly dropped.

Headline results

B1Is the g2 search range the problem?It binds, but it costs nothing. On the PRE-exclusion fit the chosen g2 does not move in 25/25 cells when the range is widened 16x. The g2 the pipeline returns is a POST-exclusion refit, though, and that one sits on the ceiling in 24/25 cells - so the earlier unqualified 'never binds' was narrowed in Step 205. Un-pinning it is a wash (-0.28pp, 12W/13L). g2 is still a large lever (9.1pp mean AUROC swing) and the criterion leaves 1.21pp on the table, pointing the wrong way in 2/25 cells. It is NOT the component-count dial - that is lambda2_threshold (exp07).
CR2 — does the 2-eigenvector rule help?No, it hurts: -3.67pp mean / -2.36pp median, 3W/21L over 25 test sets, p=0.0001. The earlier +0.5pp was measured with the g2 range capped, i.e. confounded by the dial it was testing.
CR4 — does the absolute loss help?No reliable effect: -1.29pp mean but +0.07pp median, 13W/12L, p=0.62. The mean is tail-driven. Never actually measured before — the prior number was a different estimator on the L-SML path.
CIs being faithful better?No, but weakly: fully faithful 0.6910 vs legacy 0.7392 — -4.81pp mean but only -0.18pp median, 10W/15L, p=0.173. No configuration of 64 beats GOOD_6 (unit 0.7594, eigen 0.7548, complete 0.7582).
B2Does clustering isolate the assumption violation?WITHDRAWN. The original 2.03x looked decisive but was a confound: fit error is essentially pair correlation (Spearman 0.870). Matched on correlation decile the ratio collapses to 0.996, a random partition gives 0.977, and magnitude-only clustering separates it BETTER (3.41x) than L-SML.
DDoes dropping the corrupted equations help?No. Both mechanism gates fail — rank agreement +0.160 -> +0.041 against a +0.186 bar, top-5 overlap unchanged — and performance drops -4.46pp mean / -3.18pp median, 9W/16L, p=0.030.
ECan structure replace the hand-tuned polarities?Yes for the 42 per-feature signs: deriving them from the estimated correlations scores 0.7551 vs 0.7405 (+1.46pp, 20W/5L, p=0.000).
ECan we drop the anchor too?No, and provably not: a global flip of every feature leaves the estimate bit-identical, so the global direction is not recoverable from the covariance structure at all. The rule that tried was wrong in 25/25 cells.
EHow many hand signs are wrong?15 of 30 pool features have oriented AUROC below 0.5 — not the 3 first reported, which came from inspecting only a top-6 list. Structure recovers the EMPIRICAL direction on 91.8% of features (p=0.000); agreement with our DECLARED signs is 56.1%, at the 57.5% chance level for this statistic (p=0.88) and therefore not a result.
205Is the 2-eigenvector rule what hurts?No. lambda2_frac is tightly clustered (0.094-0.233, median 0.144) just above the hardcoded 0.1, so the threshold flips 24/25 cells at once - yet removing the second component everywhere buys only +0.43pp. AND the -3.67pp that Phase C reports for this factor is a factorial MAIN EFFECT, marginalised over 32 other-factor combinations: at the DEPLOYED configuration the same switch is -0.43pp mean / +0.07pp median, 15W/9L, p=0.162. Do not quote a main effect as a deployed cost.

The honest headline

Every faithful flag hurts or does nothing, the clustered variant fails, the anchor-free rung is impossible, and no configuration reaches the existing reference subset. One-component U-PCR is exactly the first principal component of the surviving features, so the entire rho / g2 / projection-residual apparatus enters only through which features it excludes. U-PCR's estimation machinery is inert on our data; what mattered was feature orientation and feature exclusion. The orientation result in Phase E is the finding worth carrying forward.

The loading scale is reported three ways, never chosen

All of this sits on top of a correction to the L-SML residual, which returned a unit-length loading vector where the theory requires the loadings to reproduce the covariance — so a perfectly clustered block scored as an increasingly bad fit the tighter it got. SPEC_residual_scaling_fix.md pre-registered one fix (eigen); it fails that spec's own unit check and drops 6 of 25 test sets to 2 clusters. A completion-based estimator (complete) is exact on the unit checks — but it is also the only scale under which the clustered variant runs everywhere, i.e. the convenient answer. Rather than adjudicate that, every scale-dependent number here is reported under all three. See results/residual_scaling/.

What was withdrawn or dropped

  • B2's premise — the 2.03x same-vs-cross gap is a confound; it does not survive matching on correlation magnitude.
  • "Three features are mis-signed" — fifteen are.
  • "Structure recovers 56% of our hand polarities" — that is chance for this statistic.
  • A standalone scale-ratio sweep — Phase B1 removed its premise; it survives only as one factor in the Phase C factorial, where it acts through the abstention threshold rather than through g2.
  • All pooled p-values — main effects are now tested over 25 independent test sets, not 800 pooled rows.
