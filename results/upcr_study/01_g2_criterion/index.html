Does the g2 criterion work on our data?

U-PCR study, Phase B1 — the paper's Figure 1, reproduced

In short

  • On the pre-exclusion fit drawn below, the legacy search range was pinned at its own ceiling in 0/25 cells — but the g2 the pipeline actually returns is a post-exclusion refit, and that one sits on the ceiling in 24/25. Un-pinning it is a wash (−0.28pp, 12W/13L), so the range costs ~nothing either way.
  • Across the full range, g2 moves AUROC by 9.11pp on average (max 34.53pp).
  • Spearman(residual, AUROC) = -0.833; it should be negative, and is in 22/25 cells.
  • THE RANGE DOES NOT BIND ON THE PRE-EXCLUSION FIT - WHICH IS THE ONLY FIT THIS FIGURE DRAWS. On the full pool the residual minimum sits at q~0.01-0.08 in all 25 cells, far below the legacy 0.25 ceiling, and widening the range 16x moves the chosen q in 0/25 cells. BUT THE DEPLOYED g2 IS A DIFFERENT FIT: upcr_fit excludes weak experts and RECALCULATES rho and g2 on the 12 survivors of ~29, and there g2 sits exactly on the ceiling in 24/25 cells (g2/var_y median 1.0000). So an unqualified 'the range never binds' is WRONG - it binds on the estimate the pipeline actually returns, and Step 204's B1 is narrowed to the pre-exclusion fit accordingly (Step 205). The PRACTICAL conclusion survives: un-pinning it (scale_ratio 0.25 -> 1.0 with the difficulty gate off) is -0.28pp, 12W/13L, a wash, so var_y=0.25 still costs ~nothing. And g2 is NOT the component-count dial that was suspected: auto_components keys off lambda2_frac > lambda2_threshold, computed before g2 and independently of it (median lambda2_frac 0.1435 against a threshold of 0.1 - that threshold is the real dial, see exp07). g2 IS a large lever (9.11pp mean AUROC swing, 34.53pp max) and the criterion mostly points the right way (wrong direction in 2/25 cells), but it leaves 1.21pp on the table against the oracle q. AUROC is strictly monotone in q in only 6/25 cells (an earlier version of this script reported 24/25 by computing monotonicity in RES rather than in q), so the curve does have interior structure - it is simply structure this criterion does not exploit.

What this figure is

The paper picks g2 — the shared-signal level — by minimising a computable residual, and validates that choice in its Figure 1 by drawing the unobservable error curve beside it. This is that figure on our data, with AUROC standing in for the paper's MSE. Labels draw the AUROC curve only; nothing is fitted with them. The x-axis is q in units of the covariance diagonal, so 0.25 is the ceiling our code has been using and 1.0 is the paper's calibration.
