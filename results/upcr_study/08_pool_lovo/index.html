U-PCR study, exp08 — does removing weak views raise AUROC on the rho-polarity path?

In short

  • Best held-out result across four drop thresholds: -0.000pp at 0.2pp (0W/1L/24T, p = 0.317).
  • WS3 asked this on the L-SML path and answered no. This asks it on upcr.rho_polarities (0.7551), which the bench cannot reach: eval_subset_flex(fusion='upcr') runs the legacy upcr_fuse (0.7392), not this recipe.
  • Why pruning hurts: U-PCR's own exclusion is data-dependent, so removing a zero-weight view is not a no-op. Where the survivor set is unchanged the prediction holds — 91% exact no-ops, mean |delta| 0.035pp. Where removal shifts which OTHER views survive (56 of 193 pairs), 0% are no-ops and mean |delta| is 0.656pp.
  • Held-out deltas only. In-sample numbers are a ceiling, not a result.
