Every label-free attempt stays near L-SML

We tried eight ways to make label-free fusion use the contrasts LR uses. None gains more than 0.7 AUC points. Only rows that use labels reach LR.

Show numbers
What we tried

Weight contrasts inside L-SML clusters, using the other clusters, pseudo-labels, third moments, sparsity of errors, or agreement between clusters. Or feed LR's pairs to the methods as ready-made differences.

Why it cannot work this way

LR's gain sits in exact, signed weights between channels that repeat each other. Without labels those weights are not identifiable: the error label explains only about 1% of the correlation between clusters, and the rest is shared noise.

What the labels buy

With true labels, the same channels reach 0.810 by conditioning. With labels choosing the pairs, even a plain average gains 1.6 points.

Limit: each attempt shows its best setting, chosen after seeing labels, so every blue row is an upper bound. Rows marked “uses labels” are references, not label-free methods.
