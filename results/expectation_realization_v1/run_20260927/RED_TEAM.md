## What the original report would have missed

1. **"Averaging" was a handicapped control.** The protocol said every column is risk-oriented, yet two change channels are anti-oriented within answer (known since Step 438). The block_equal − equal gain is a down-weighting of those two channels, not an effect of the blocks.
2. **No position-only reference.** Step index alone scores 0.662 within answer on PRMBench. A derivative channel carries part of that positional prior, so its margin over CT7 must be read against a position-adjusted version (+0.0026).
3. **The single post-hoc channel beats every fusion.** realized_drv alone (0.7957) is above all fused arms (best 0.7805).
4. **A misquoted range in stage A**: 0.69–0.76, where the correct range is 0.65–0.76 (mean 0.69).
