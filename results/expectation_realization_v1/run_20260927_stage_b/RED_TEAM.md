## What the original design missed

- **No whole-answer label-swap null.** The within-answer shuffle null alone cannot show that a within-answer gain is positional. Position of the first error (not the channel content) explains the S_equal gain on the original bank.
- **The frozen tie-breaking change was not checked against the premise.** The protocol assumed the stage-A family partition, but I changed the tie handling without re-checking it label-free before freezing. Amendment B1 was needed after the smoke.
- **All PRMBench evidence is one backbone** (prmbench_qwen3_8b).
