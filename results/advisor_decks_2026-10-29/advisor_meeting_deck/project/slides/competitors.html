How many benchmarks and models each paper reports, its label use, and how we compare with it.
Answering “why were some papers missing”: the head-to-head work so far covered the three that report on PRMBench with a protocol we can recompute (PRISM, CROP, SLIDER). Of the other four: R-PRM is a table-to-table leaderboard row (PRMBench 66.8 vs AUTO 69.9–70.2; on ProcessBench 70.4 vs Qwen-PRM-72B 78.2); ARES used an unknown PRMBench subset and a CV-tuned threshold, and its own appendix shows the PRM alone beating it — we can run its 5-fold threshold protocol on CPU; Mind the Gap reports exact-first-step accuracy on ProcessBench and final-answer selection, not PRMBench — our earlier reproduction (Step 449) matched its pattern; BR2-PRM is multimodal with no code, so it is positioning only. Models = PRMs or scoring models; ours are 5 PRMs, each with backbone and standalone telemetry.
