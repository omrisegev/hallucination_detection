Where it stands, and what to try next

Claim we can make

One label-free recipe beats every PRM, PRISM and R-PRM on PRMBench, and matches CROP’s supervised fusion there. It is strongest on redundancy and circular errors.

Limit

Out of domain it loses to a strong PRM; a supervised LR does not fix that. The flag rule can never say “no error”, which costs most on ProcessBench and CRV.

Next

A “no error” decision from the raw, unnormalized signal; a label-free test of PRM strength; ARES and Mind the Gap protocols.

Why raw signals for the no-error decision: we standardize every answer by itself, which deletes the answer-level level of uncertainty — exactly what an “is there any error” decision needs. In September the no-error gate was the weakest component (3–5 points lost on every ProcessBench cell), and our original final-answer work detected wrong answers from raw answer-level features (AUROC 0.76–0.90). Controls needed: answer length, and PRMBench (96% of answers have an error) is not the place to measure it.
