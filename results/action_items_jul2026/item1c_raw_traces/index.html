What the raw data actually looks like

One level below the features: the questions, what the model emitted, and the token-entropy trace the 30 views are computed from. TriviaQA / OPT-30B (base) against TriviaQA / Qwen3-8B.

The weak cell’s traces are malformed, and it is a generation defect, not a fusion one. Every one of its 5,000 traces is exactly 64 tokens — 99.7% sit exactly at max_new_tokens, so not one generation stopped on its own. 99.8% run past the answer and begin inventing a new few-shot Question: block. The answer itself is a median of 3 tokens — about 5% of the trace. The other ~95% is the model talking to itself, and all 30 features are computed over the whole thing.

3. Does cropping to the answer recover the signal?

A direct test of the parsing hypothesis, needing no re-generation: recompute simple entropy features over the full trace and over the answer span only, and compare AUROC. If the run-on is what is destroying the signal, cropping should help on the weak cell and do nothing on the strong one. AUROC is oracle-oriented here (these are throwaway diagnostics, not deployed views).

Result: the best single-feature gain from cropping is +29.7pp on the weak cell. Cropping recovers real signal, so the run-on is doing measurable damage and a re-run with a proper stop sequence is worth it.

4. Is the gain just answer length again?

It has to be asked. Cropping makes the trace length vary with the answer, and the last cell we headlined (spilled_triviaqa_llama8b) turned out to be a pure length detector. So: does answer length predict correctness on its own, and does the cropped feature still work inside a fixed answer length?

TriviaQA / OPT-30B (base)TriviaQA / Qwen3-8B
answer length alone0.52040.6069
max entropy on the crop0.82660.8090
|corr(feature, length)|0.1620.288
max entropy WITHIN fixed answer length0.8217 (4,665/5,000 samples)0.8013 (3,258/5,000 samples)

Not leakage. Answer length alone is 0.5204 on the weak cell — chance — and the cropped feature still scores 0.8217 (4,665/5,000 samples) when compared only against answers of the same length. The signal is in the entropy of the answer tokens, not in how many there are.

5. What this suggests

This cell was generated with a base (non-instruct) model in a few-shot prompt, at max_new_tokens = 64, with no stop sequence at the newline. A base model in that setup does not stop after answering — it continues the pattern. The strong cell used an instruct model that emits an answer and halts, which is why its median trace is 11 tokens and none of it is filler.

So before concluding anything about features or fusion on this cell, the generation should be re-run with a stop sequence on the newline (and on Question:), which is a cheap change to the preset. Until then its numbers describe a parsing artifact, not the method.
