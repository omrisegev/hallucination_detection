1. Does the gap exist before any fusion runs?

The question this whole page exists to answer. If the features on the weak cell are simply less informative, then a supervised model with full access to the labels cannot do well either — and the gap is in the raw material, not in the label-free machinery. The supervised ceiling moves +11.4pp across this pair, against a label-free gap of +22.5pp, so on the order of 51% of the gap is already present in the features before any fusion, selection or orientation runs. The best single view alone differs by +19.6pp.

Treat that percentage as a direction, not a measurement. Bootstrapping it in the Step-215 review gave intervals wide enough to include 100% on two of the three pairs, and swapping in a different but equally defensible partner cell moved the TriviaQA figure across the range −2% to 60%. What survives is the sign: some of the gap is in the features, and not all of it.

On the weak cell the supervised model reaches +16.1pp above what we deliver; on the strong cell +5.0pp.

3. How the views correlate with one another

Both L-SML and U-PCR are built on one assumption: a single latent variable — correctness — drives every view, so the off-diagonal correlation should look like ρiρj, a rank-1 matrix. Everything they estimate (the grouping, the weights, the polarities) is read off that matrix. Three panels: the total correlation, the within-class correlation (class means removed — the part the label does not explain), and their difference, which is the only part the latent-variable model can legitimately use.

A note on the third panel: total minus within-class is a difference of two correlation matrices, not an exact variance decomposition — removing the class means also shrinks each view's variance. It is a consistent, comparable measure of how much extra co-movement the label accounts for, and both panels are computed identically, but do not read an individual entry as a between-class covariance.

Do not read mean |excess| as a second, independent finding. For two classes with prior π, the excess is algebraically Eij = wij(gigj−1) + κdidjgigj with gi = 1/√(1+κdi²). Predicting E from the Cohen's d values in section 2, the within-class matrix and π alone reproduces the measured E entrywise at correlation ≥ +0.99997 on every cell here. So it is section 2 restated, multiplied by the class prior — not a separate mechanism. It also carries a variance-rescaling term of opposite sign worth 44–62% of the label term, so an individual entry is a partially cancelled residual. This was written up as an independent finding in Step 214 and withdrawn in Step 215.
