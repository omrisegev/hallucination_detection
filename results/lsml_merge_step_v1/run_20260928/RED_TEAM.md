## What the author's preliminary report got wrong or left out
- It attributed the B32 loss (-0.0239) to "L-SML with the step" although the step never fired on B32.
- It reported the central losses as significant on 4 of 5 banks without saying they are mostly positional (B13, B20, B51 fully;
  B32 about 2/3). It did flag the position-adjusted reversal, as a hypothesis; the red team confirms that reversal on B20, B32, B51.
- It did not say that on B13 the L-SML weights are equal-per-group by L-SML's own 3-unit guard.
- The earlier diagnosis and message to Omri called the absorption ratio size-free; it is not (the merge threshold depends on the
  smaller group's size).
- "Merges the level halves and nothing else" holds on the raw DS-filtered partitions, not on the position-adjusted or band-rule ones.
