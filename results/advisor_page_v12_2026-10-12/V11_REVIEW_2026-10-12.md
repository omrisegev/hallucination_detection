## 2. Conclusions for the advisors

Each conclusion gives the number and the file.

**C1. The overall tie with PRM-7B comes partly from PRM's default threshold.**
- At the default rules, PRMScore is a tie: L-SML 65.38, PRM-7B 65.46. The paired difference is −0.08, 95% CI [−0.77, +0.64] (`report/legacy/PAIRED_DECISION_ANALYSIS.json`).
- PRM's 0.5 threshold flags only 8.65% of steps. Our Q80 flags 19.9% (`controls/EXACT_DECISION_RULES.csv`).
- At the same flag budget (about 18% for both), PRM gets 68.31 and L-SML gets 65.69 (`controls/DIAGNOSTIC_CEILINGS.json`, `global_threshold`). So PRM ranks better overall by about 2.6 points when both get their best global threshold.
- The page states the +2.857 oracle gain for PRM, but it does not draw this conclusion. Add it to section 01.

**C2. Step position explains a large part of the overall AUC differences.**
- Without controls, PRM leads L-SML by 2.20 AUC points [1.50, 2.90]. L-SML leads the plain average by 0.84 [0.59, 1.08].
- When we match error and valid steps on relative position (4,691 of 6,030 answers), both differences go away:
  - L-SML − PRM = +1.01 [−0.16, +2.22].
  - L-SML − plain average = −0.30 [−0.81, +0.23].
- With position + length matching (3,021 answers), the plain average (0.710) is equal to or higher than L-SML (0.706) and IU-PCR (0.703).
- Source: `controls/PAIRED_AUC_CONTRASTS.csv`, `controls/NUISANCE_CONTROLS.csv`.
- Meaning: on the full benchmark, the fusion gain over the plain average is not robust to the position control. The matched set is a subset, so this is a caution, not a final result. The page does not show the plain average in its control table, so the reader cannot see this. Add it.

**C3. Redundancy (NR) is our robust strength. Circular (NCL) is not proven.**
- NR, after position + length matching: L-SML − PRM = +19.7 AUC points [15.9, 23.6], 465 answers. With repetition matching too: +18.4 [13.8, 22.9].
- NR is also the only type where L-SML beats the plain average after controls: +2.6 [0.8, 4.4].
- NCL after matching: +4.3 [−0.5, +9.2]. The interval crosses zero.
- PRM is much stronger on three Soundness/Sensitivity types (raw AUC, L-SML − PRM): confidence −11.3, counterfactual −12.1, deception −14.3 points.
- Source: `controls/PAIRED_AUC_CONTRASTS.csv`.

**C4. The decision problem is per-answer allocation, not the global level.**
- A better global threshold adds almost nothing for us: Q80 65.38, best global cut 65.69 (+0.31). Q80 is already near our best global cut (19.9% vs 18.1% flagged).
- With the true number of errors per answer (same ranking): 71.73 (+6.3).
- With perfect ranking at our own counts: 81.82 (+16.4).
- Source: `controls/DIAGNOSTIC_CEILINGS.json`. The ceilings use labels. They are not additive.
- So the storyline "the ranking is close; the decision is the bottleneck" needs a change. The global cutoff is not the problem. The number of flags per answer is a problem, and the ranking is also a problem.

**C5. R2_COUNT gives abstention almost free of cost.**
- R2_COUNT lowers false flags on synthetic clean answers from 99.5% to 58.6%. The PRMScore cost is only −0.05 (65.33 vs 65.38).
- ENTROPY_GATE_Q80 gives MS 97.6 (Q80: 88.4) and an almost zero count bias (−0.03), with a cost of −0.5 PRMScore.
- PRM-7B falsely flags 19.0% of clean answers.
- Source: `controls/EXACT_DECISION_RULES.csv`.
- The page title says "with an overall cost". For R2_COUNT, the cost is close to zero. Say this.

**C6. The MoM count estimate mostly copies the vote construction.**
- Each L-SML group vote marks the top ceil(20%) of steps in every answer. That is 23.0% of all steps by construction.
- Group MoM estimates 22.9% error prevalence (truth 14.0%). The quota flags 22.98% of steps.
- So the estimate is close to the fixed vote rate, not to the true rate. This supports family A in Codex's count-estimator prompt (votes before within-answer standardization). This is our inference, not a tested result.
- Source: `statistics/ESTIMATOR_ACCURACY.csv`, `statistics/MOM_QUOTA.csv`.

**C7. LR: the gap grows with bank size on AUC, but we lead LR on top1 in the small banks.**
- AUC gap (LR minus the better label-free method): 0.021 at N16, peak 0.063 at N40, 0.051 at N56. The correlation with bank size is 0.89, but the trend is not monotone (`lr/METRICS.csv`).
- Top1: we lead LR up to N25. At N16, L-SML − LR = +1.97 points [0.96, 2.96]. At N56, LR leads by 6.0 points (`lr/TOP1_PAIRED_CONTRASTS.csv`).
- LR puts +0.44 and −0.44 on two digit channels with training correlation 0.9998. This pair carries 88% of the absolute LR weight. Cosine between LR and our weights is about 0.03 (`lr/WEIGHT_STABILITY.csv`, `lr/WEIGHT_AGREEMENT.csv`).
- Removing the uncertainty family (F1) or the surprise family (F2) costs more AUC than removing the digit family at N16: −1.58 / −1.56 vs −1.00 points (`lr/ABLATIONS.csv`). No paired test compares them.

**C8. The prefix fix causes the AUC decline. It is not a step-0 effect. mask1 is neutral.**
- Prefix fix: L-SML −1.59 AUC points [−1.84, −1.34]. Without step 0, the decline is −2.24, so it is larger, not smaller.
- mask1 alone: −0.07 [−0.15, +0.01].
- Source: `controls/PREFIX_MASK_CONTRASTS.csv`.

**C9. Two 8 October claims must be withdrawn.**
- "realized_drv leads 6 of 8 error types": on the corrected input it leads 0 of 8 (`statistics/CHANNEL_BY_TYPE.csv`).
- "Every method in about 40 literature rows is weaker on Simplicity": Table 3 has 15 model rows, and 10 of them have Simplicity below Soundness and Sensitivity (`report/paper_table3.csv`). The page already withdraws this claim.

**C10. Internal validation holds, but it is a weak test.**
- Held-out half: N16 IU-PCR AUC 0.786, top1 60.1% (`validation/HALF_TEST.csv`). The donor choice beat L-SML by only 0.001 AUC.
- Leave-one-type-out changes per-type AUC by at most 0.001. The fusers do not use labels, so this result is expected. It is not strong evidence of transfer.

## 5. Takeaway bullets per figure (to place under each figure)

Each figure gets 2-3 bullets. The numbers come from the files named in sections 2-4.

**Radar and category table (section 03)**
- We are best in Simplicity: NR 70.0 and NCL 68.0, against 49.0 and 55.0 for PRM-7B. No row in paper Table 3 is higher.
- PRM-7B is better in Soundness (71.0 vs 66.9) and Sensitivity (75.5 vs 69.3).
- Our MS is low (88.4 vs 99.9), because Q80 flags at least one step in almost every clean answer.

**Prefix and mask attribution (section 02 table; `prefix_mask_ablation.svg`)**
- The prefix fix lowers L-SML AUC by 1.6 points, and the interval excludes zero.
- The decline is larger without step 0 (−2.2), so it is not a first-token artifact.
- mask1 changes AUC by less than 0.1 point; the interval includes zero.

**Historical bank panels (section 04, digit-free and with digits)**
- LR is above both label-free methods in every bank.
- The AUC gap is smallest at N14/N16 (about 0.021) and largest at N40 (0.064).
- The new group-strict fits repeat these values: N16 LR 0.802 vs 0.801 historical.

**New OOF gap across ten banks**
- The new group-strict fits repeat the historical gaps to within 0.001 at N16.
- The AUC gap grows with bank size (r = 0.89), but it peaks at N40, not N56.
- On top1 the result is opposite up to N25: at N16, L-SML leads LR by 2.0 points [1.0, 3.0].

**Crossover: half-bank selection × weighting (N16, N56)**
- LR does best on its own half: 0.797 at N16, vs 0.788 / 0.791 on the label-free halves.
- On the same LR half, label-free weighting stays well below LR: 0.774 (L-SML) vs 0.797.
- The LR half raises L-SML AUC at N16 (+1.0) but lowers its top1 (−4.5) and lowers AUC at N56 (−0.6). It is not a label-free fix.

**N16 weights**
- LR puts +0.44 and −0.44 on two digit channels that are almost copies (r = 0.9998). The two weights cancel.
- All other LR weights are 0.025 or less.
- L-SML and IU-PCR spread the weight evenly (largest about 0.10). Their weights agree with each other (cosine 0.96) and not with LR (cosine 0.03).

**N56 weights**
- LR keeps the same digit contrast (+0.30 / −0.31).
- LR has 21 negative weights of 56; IU-PCR has none.
- The label-free weights agree with LR even less than at N16 (cosine about 0.02).

**Weight mass by feature family**
- At N16, LR puts 89% of its absolute weight on the digit family; L-SML puts 15% there.
- L-SML and IU-PCR put the most weight on uncertainty (F1): 48% and 44%.
- Weight mass shows where the coefficients are, not how much each family predicts. See the omission figure.

**Family omission**
- At N16, removing uncertainty (F1) or surprise (F2) costs about 1.6 AUC points; removing digits costs 1.0.
- Removing only one channel of the digit pair costs nothing, because the pair works as a contrast.
- At N56, F1 still costs the most (−1.55); rhythm (F5) costs almost nothing in both banks.

**Four-way top1 outcomes by error type (N16)**
- L-SML alone finds the error in 485 answers that LR misses; LR alone finds 366. Net +119 for L-SML.
- We win most on redundancy (90 vs 27), domain (79 vs 32) and circular (77 vs 42).
- LR wins on confidence (60 vs 28) and deception (75 vs 58).

**Nuisance controls (NR, NCL, all)**
- On all answers, position matching removes PRM's AUC lead and our lead over the plain average.
- On redundancy, our lead over PRM stays at about +18 to +20 AUC points under every control.
- On circular, our lead shrinks to +4.3 points, and the interval crosses zero.

**Budget curves**
- At the same flag rate, PRM's curve is above ours: 68.3 vs 65.7 at about 18% flagged.
- Our Q80 point (20%) is almost at the top of our curve. A better global threshold adds only 0.3 points.
- PRM's native 0.5 threshold flags only 8.7% of steps. That is why its default score equals ours.

**Conditional correlations (N16)**
- Features correlate more on error steps than on valid steps: mean |r| 0.30 vs 0.24.
- The largest change is chosen_surprisal with energy_level: 0.50 on error steps vs 0.17 on valid steps.
- Most pairs change by less than 0.1. Small changes do not prove conditional independence.

**Channel × error-type heatmap**
- No channel wins every type. Uncertainty channels lead redundancy (0.84), domain (0.81) and circular (0.77). digit_spread leads deception (0.73).
- Step index alone reaches 0.87 on confidence, more than any channel. It is near chance on domain (0.54) and circular (0.57).
- One channel (rolling spectral entropy) is below 0.5 in all eight types, so its label-free sign is wrong. realized_drv no longer leads any type.

**Tables without figures (add 2 bullets each)**
- Estimators: all estimators give too high an error rate. Group MoM gives 22.9%; the truth is 14.0%. This is close to the 23.0% that each vote marks by construction.
- MoM quota: PRMScore falls to 64.2 (Q80: 65.4), and 99.9% of clean answers still get a flag (Q80: 99.5%).
- Validation: on the held-out half, N16 IU-PCR keeps AUC 0.786 and top1 60.1%. This is internal validation on data that we already looked at.
