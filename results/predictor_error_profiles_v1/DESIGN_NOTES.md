# Scope amendments and interpretation

The initial protocol was saved before the six diagnostic outcome summaries.
After those plots and the user's clarification, analysis was broadened to the
11 available aligned full-score archives, with a full-array common-anchor
check for each source. The broad inventory and before-gate885-row cohort are
secondary descriptive analyses, not independently confirmed discoveries.

The immediately preceding correct-step boundary control was added AFTER the
initial event plot showed a common onset spike. It is explicitly post hoc.
Correctness here means the benchmark's first-error convention, not an
independent semantic adjudication of every preceding step. The text example
math-404 illustrates why the distinction matters. No labels were changed.

The two6-diagnostic/2-contrast bootstrap panels each use10000 source-group
draws and CI99.583333%. There is no claim of family-wise error control over
both panels, historical selection, plots and later sensitivity checks jointly.
When a retained matching stratum has an empty group in a bootstrap draw,
its fixed harmonic weight is removed and the others renormalized. The audit
reports how often this occurred (770 initial,592 broad). Missing history is
excluded only from the feature-change statistic, with its own denominators.

Means aligned to step START mix durations and available histories. Positive
offsets can leave the selected step, including entering the first error from
the preceding-correct-step control. Coverage is saved per bin; first4 means
are separately restricted to each actual step. No erroneous token is assumed.

Shared low residuals or predictor agreement do not prove that a semantic
error has no signal, and high disagreement does not identify an error. Cohort
membership already depends on related scores. The observations motivate
isolating representation versus step-readout limitations before another
weight sweep; they are not a new scoring policy.
