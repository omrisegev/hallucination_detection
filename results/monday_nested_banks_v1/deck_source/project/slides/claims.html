What the evidence supports

We can say

  • Label-free fitting matches a supervised PRM on the top step on the best development bank; about 3 AUC points lower across banks
  • Above our Mind the Gap reproduction on both benchmarks
  • Over an oriented plain average, fusion adds at most ~0.01 AUC, only on small banks; two added channels add 0.025
  • Estimators rank channels well (ρ ≈ 0.8) but overstate the error rate 1.5–2.2×
  • Socratic transfer follows the development ranking; Hard2Verify does not

We cannot say

  • Better than supervised PRMs, or "tied" on AUC
  • A winner among the four fusion methods
  • That N16's external result is a new test (it is old B16)
  • Anything about bank size from one nesting order
  • That confidently stated errors are a hard limit of label-free methods (position explains part)
