The pipeline, fitted on each answer set without labels

1 · Telemetry

Teacher-forced token probabilities of the given answer

2 · Channels

11 to 56 per-step uncertainty features (a bank)

3 · Answer z-score

Each channel rescaled within its own answer

4 · Binary tails

Top 20% steps of an answer vote "error" (±1)

5 · DS filter

Drop channels estimated worse than chance

6 · Partition

L-SML clustering of dependent channels, then merge (≥3 groups)

7 · Within group

MEAN, HEM weights, or TCI triplet

8 · Between groups

MoM estimate of each group's accuracy → weights

9 · Step score

Weighted sum of continuous group scores

10 · Decision

q80 / R2 count / entropy gate

Binary votes feed the estimators only (steps 5, 6, 8); the continuous z-scores are what gets combined. Suffix _cc = channel votes, quota per cell; _ga = group votes, quota per answer.
