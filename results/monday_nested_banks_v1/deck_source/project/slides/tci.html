TCI triplets (Prop. 2.4): detects dependence, does not transfer

Rule

Under conditional independence, T_ijk / C_ij = α·t_k for every pair (i, j). In each group with ≥ 4 members, test every triplet plus an anchor (mean of the other groups):

V = mean_s [1 − (t·c)² / (|t|²|c|²)], t_pq = E[z_p z_q z_s], c_pq = C_pq. Keep the triplet with the smallest V; MoM between groups.

Origin

Proposition 2.4 of Lee et al., "FUSE: Ensembling Verifiers with Zero Labeled Data", 2026 (a diagnostic there). The selection rule is Omri's (5 Oct).

What it detects and what it buys

Marks violate conditional independence 2,080× (B13) and 3,228× (B32) relative to a CI null: the statistic sees our groups.

PRMBench vs MEAN_MOM: +0.0026 AUC on 8 banks, but +0.0001 on the 6 banks not used to design it.

ProcessBench: −0.010 [−0.014, −0.005]. Externally (B16), below the DS-filter average on both Socratic cells.
