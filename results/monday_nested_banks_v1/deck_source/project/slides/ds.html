Dawid–Skene EM: a filter that is right about signs, wrong about levels

Model

P(f) = Σ_y P(y) · Π_i P(f_i | y): channels independent given the step's true class. EM, 5 starts, best likelihood.

ψ_i = P(vote error | error) · η_i = P(vote ok | ok) · π_i = (ψ_i + η_i)/2

Origin

Dawid & Skene, J. R. Stat. Soc. C 28(1), 1979. The keep-rule is ours (Steps 451/457).

How we use it

Fit per cell on all steps' votes; keep channels with π̂ > 0.5. Plain average of survivors = DS_FILTER_EQ.

The DS filter works as an orientation detector: the channels it removes are precisely the ones pointing the wrong way. Its numeric estimates are biased because the conditional-independence assumption fails: several level channels share a factor, so DS sees them agree and overestimates their sensitivity and the error prevalence (0.31 vs a true 0.14).
