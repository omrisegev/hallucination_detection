SML and L-SML: good at ranking channels, not at weighting them

Idea and formula

If channels are conditionally independent, the off-diagonal covariance of votes is rank one: q_ij = t_i·t_j with t_i ∝ (2π_i − 1). The leading eigenvector ranks reliability.

L-SML: cluster dependent channels first (score s_ij = Σ|r_ij·r_kl − r_il·r_kj|), SML within groups, then SML across groups; sign set by the anchor q15_H1.

Origin

Parisi, Strino, Nadler, Kluger, PNAS 2014 (SML) · Jaffe, Fetaya, Nadler, Jiang, Kluger, 2016 (L-SML, dependent classifiers)

Estimate vs truth and outcome

Ranking only, no prevalence or ψ/η. Spearman with true reliability 0.799 (channel votes), 0.730 (group votes).

Full L-SML fusion below the plain average on 8 of 8 banks (0.7532 vs 0.7656 AUC).

Why: dependence between groups survives (class-conditional |r| 0.14–0.31, worst pairs 0.72–0.86); adding 21 weak channels dropped AUC 0.7645 → 0.6724.

Today L-SML is used only to form groups (next slide).
