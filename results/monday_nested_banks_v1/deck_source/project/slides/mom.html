Tensor method of moments: closed-form accuracies, biased levels

Formula

E[z_i z_j] = t_i t_j and E[z_i z_j z_k] = α · t_i t_j t_k, with α = −2b / √(1 − b²) (b = class imbalance).

α by least squares over distinct triples → b → ψ_i, η_i in closed form; no EM, no local optima.

Origin

Jaffe, Nadler, Kluger, "Estimating the Accuracies of Multiple Classifiers Without Labeled Data", AISTATS 2015 (Algorithm 1).

How we use it

Between-group weights w_g = log(ψη / ((1−ψ)(1−η))), clipped at 0, in every *_MOM recipe.
